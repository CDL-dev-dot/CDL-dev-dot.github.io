Questions, answered.

Which test will I take — 2025 or 2008?

If you filed Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025, you take the 2025 test (128 questions; up to 20 asked, 12 to pass). If you filed before that date, you take the 2008 test (100 questions; up to 10 asked, 6 to pass). Civics Prep covers both — pick your version once and everything adapts.

Is the content really accurate?

Every question and answer is taken word-for-word from official USCIS study materials, which are U.S. government works in the public domain. Answers that change with elections are never hardcoded — the app points you to the official USCIS update page so you never study an outdated name.

Is it really free?

Every official question and every study tool you see today is free, and will stay free. If optional extras ever appear, nothing you already have will be taken away.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All questions, translations, and memory tips are on your device. Study on the subway, on a plane, anywhere.

Is this an official USCIS app?

No. Civics Prep is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USCIS or any government agency.
