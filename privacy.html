Privacy Policy — Civics Prep

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Summary

Civics Prep does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Period.

What data the app handles

Your study progress (questions practiced, mock test results, wrong-answer list) and your settings (test version, display language) are stored only on your device, using Apple's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to our servers (we have none), and is deleted when you delete the app.

What we do NOT do

  • No account or registration
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No third-party data sharing
  • No network transmission of your study data

External links

The app links to official USCIS web pages (uscis.gov) for answer updates. Opening those links takes you to the USCIS website, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if optional features requiring network access are added), the updated policy will be posted at this address before the change takes effect.
